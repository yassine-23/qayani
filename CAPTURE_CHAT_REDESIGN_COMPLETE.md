## 🎨 Design Principles Applied

### **Typography:**
- Ultra-light weights (font-light)
- Large sizes (text-7xl, text-8xl)
- Generous line-height and letter-spacing
- tracking-tight for headings
- tracking-wide for body text

### **Layout:**
- Maximum whitespace
- 40-unit margins between sections (mb-40)
- Clean borders (border-gray-200)
- Rounded corners (rounded-3xl)
- No gradients or colors (gray-scale only with minimal black accents)

### **Colors:**
- Primary: Gray-900 (black)
- Secondary: Gray-600 (mid gray)
- Tertiary: Gray-500 (light gray)
- Backgrounds: White, Gray-50
- Accents: Minimal, only black

### **Animation:**
- Slow, elegant transitions (duration: 1.2s)
- Apple-style easing: [0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1]
- Subtle opacity and y-position changes
- No flashy effects

### **Components:**
- Simple dots instead of checkmarks
- Clean lists with minimal decoration
- Architectural spec sheets (label: content format)
- Border separators instead of colored backgrounds
