# Influencer Onboarding Playbook

## Target Influencer Profile (Beta Phase)

### Ideal Beta Influencer Characteristics:

**Size:**
- **Sweet Spot:** 100k-500k followers
- **Why Not Bigger:** Mega-influencers (1M+) have teams, slow decision-making, high expectations
- **Why Not Smaller:** Micro-influencers (<50k) generate insufficient test traffic

**Engagement:**
- Engagement rate: 3%+ (Instagram), 5%+ (Twitter/X), 10%+ (TikTok)
- Active community: Regular DMs, comments, questions
- **Red Flag:** Follower count high but low engagement (bought followers)

**Content Type:**
- **✅ Perfect:** Coaches, educators, self-improvement, productivity, business
- **✅ Great:** Fitness trainers, health coaches, career advisors
- **✅ Good:** Tech influencers, creators sharing expertise
- **❌ Avoid:** Entertainment-only, meme accounts, celebrity influencers

**Pain Point Fit:**
- Explicitly mentions: "I wish I could help everyone", "Sorry I can't respond to all DMs"
- Sells coaching/consulting but limited by time
- High-value knowledge that scales (not personalized 1-on-1 work)

**Platform:**
- **Primary:** Instagram, YouTube, Twitter/X
- **Why:** High text-based engagement, creator-owned audiences
- **Avoid:** TikTok-only (younger audience, less B2B fit)

### Influencer Tiers

| Tier | Followers | Priority | Expected Sessions/Week | Value |
|------|-----------|----------|------------------------|-------|
| **Tier 1** | 200k-500k | 🔴 High | 500-1,000 | Mega validation |
| **Tier 2** | 100k-200k | 🟡 Medium | 200-500 | Strong validation |
| **Tier 3** | 50k-100k | 🟢 Low | 50-200 | Early testing |

**Beta Goal:** 1 Tier 1 + 2 Tier 2 + 2 Tier 3 = 5 total influencers

## Outreach Strategy

- **Twitter/X:** Search: "I wish I could help everyone", "too many DMs"

- **LinkedIn:** Search: coaches, consultants, thought leaders

**Criteria:**
```
✅ 100k-500k followers
✅ Posts about helping followers/fans
✅ Mentions being overwhelmed by messages
✅ Sells coaching/courses (shows monetization intent)
✅ Responds to comments (shows they care about community)
```

### Phase 2: Outreach Execution (3-7 Days)

#### Outreach Channels (Best to Worst)

**1. Warm Intro (Best - 50% response rate)**
- Ask mutual connections for introduction
- LinkedIn connections
- Alumni networks
- YC network (after acceptance)

**2. Email (Good - 20% response rate)**
- Use Hunter.io or similar to find email
- Subject: "[Name], I built something for you"
- Keep under 100 words
- Include demo link

**3. Instagram DM (Medium - 10% response rate)**
- Send during US business hours (9am-5pm PT)
- Comment on recent post first
- Then send DM with message above

**4. Twitter/X DM (Medium - 10% response rate)**
- Reply to relevant tweet
- Send DM after they engage

**5. LinkedIn Message (Low - 5% response rate)**
- Only if you have LinkedIn Premium
- Otherwise, connection requests get ignored

## Common Objections & Responses

### "This feels too robotic/inauthentic"

**Response:**
"I understand. Most AI chatbots DO feel robotic. But Qayani is different because:
1. It's real-time (not video generation) — <300ms latency feels like a real conversation
2. It sounds exactly like you (we clone your voice)
3. It reacts to emotions (smiles when user is happy, frowns when sad)

Try the demo for 30 seconds — you'll see it's not like other AI tools."

### "My audience wants ME, not an AI"

**Response:**
"Totally agree. And here's the thing — they CAN'T have you 24/7. You're one person with limited time.

The options are:
1. Ignore most messages (current state — feels bad)
2. Hire VAs (expensive, don't sound like you)
3. Give them an AI that feels 80% like you (better than 0%)

Your superfans will still want 1-on-1 time with you. But the 95% who just have a quick question? They'll be thrilled to talk to your AI twin."

### "What if it says something wrong?"

**Response:**
"Great question. The AI is trained on YOUR content, so it only says things you've already said publicly. And we have guardrails to prevent it from:
- Making up facts
- Giving medical/legal advice
- Saying anything controversial

You can also review conversations and adjust the AI's knowledge base anytime. Think of it as: It's you, but with a safety filter."

### "How is this different from ChatGPT?"

**Response:**
"Three huge differences:
1. **Voice:** ChatGPT is text. Qayani is a real-time voice conversation that sounds like you.
2. **Avatar:** You see a 3D avatar that reacts to your emotions in real-time.
3. **Latency:** ChatGPT (with plugins) has 2-3 second delays. Qayani is <300ms — feels like FaceTime.

It's the difference between texting someone vs. video calling them."

### "I don't have time to set this up"

**Response:**
"We handle everything. You just need:
1. 3-5 audio samples (2 minutes total) — can be from podcasts/videos
2. 5-10 FAQs your audience asks
3. 30-minute onboarding call

That's it. We do the rest, and your twin is live same day."

### "How much does it cost?"

**Response (Beta Phase):**
"For beta influencers, it's free. We handle setup, give you unlimited conversations, and just ask for feedback + a testimonial.

After beta (Month 3), we'll have paid tiers:
- Starter: $99/mo (1,000 conversations)
- Creator: $299/mo (10,000 conversations)
- Business: $999/mo (unlimited)

But as a beta influencer, you'll get a lifetime discount (50% off)."

### "What if my fans don't like it?"

**Response:**
"If that happens, we'll shut it down immediately. No hard feelings.

But here's what we've seen so far: [show testimonials from previous beta influencers].

Fans LOVE it because they finally get to 'talk' to you. Even if they know it's AI, they appreciate the effort."
